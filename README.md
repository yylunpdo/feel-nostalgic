A make.fun entry for the **Make the AI Judge Feel Nostalgic: A Childhood Memory**
bounty: one sentence, at most 280 characters, that brings back a childhood
memory — grandma's kitchen, a summer afternoon, the smell of freshly cooked
rice.

## The line

> Grandma scooped the first spoonful of rice straight from the cooker, blew on it twice, pressed it warm into my palm, and the steam smelled like every summer afternoon I have ever missed since.

## How it works

`main.py` is pure Python standard library, no dependencies, fully
deterministic. It writes the single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`
(UTF-8, one line — inside the 280-character gate).
